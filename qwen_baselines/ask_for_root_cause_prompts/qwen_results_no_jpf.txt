Case 1: 1-lock_order_inversion_blocks-0
Root cause analysis
1. Classification
Bug type: resource_deadlock
Family: Deadlock
Deadlock type: Resource
2. Root cause
Shared state: asleepLock, monitorC
Accesses involved:

    t1 | CountingLanterns.tryToSleep | lock asleepLock, lock monitorC
    t2 | CountingLanterns.setAsleep | lock monitorC, lock asleepLock
    Missing guarantee: Consistent lock acquisition order
    Buggy interleaving:

    Thread t1 acquires asleepLock in tryToSleep.
    Thread t2 acquires monitorC in setAsleep.
    Why it fails: Thread t1 waits for monitorC held by t2, and thread t2 waits for asleepLock held by t1, causing a circular wait and deadlock.
